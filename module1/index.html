Gradient descent in one dimension

xk+1 = xk − α f ′(xk) Choose the step size α and the start x0, and watch the iterates on the graph of f and their error on a log scale. On (x − 3)2 + 4, the error is multiplied by |1 − 2α| at each step: the iterates converge, alternate, or diverge. On ex − 2x, compare with Newton's method, which converges quadratically.
